**Evaluated by P32 on 2026-10-13.** `spec/join-policy.md` ships `arrival-count`, `live-token` and
`live-token-requiring-arrival`, and all three are `satisfied` only once every opened branch has arrived,
exited or been lost. A race (*the first of three providers to answer*) or a quorum (*two of three
reviewers*) has no policy.

**The policy itself is the easy half** — a `step` that is `satisfied` at the Nth arrival is a few lines
against `JP-R3`. **The hard half is the losers.** They cannot be cancelled while queued
([S2](../scheduler-upstream.md)), so they settle after the join fired: whether those settlements are
recorded, routed, or reach the join's successor's `JP-R12` collection — and what `FD-C1`'s *the value
this node receives* is for a successor dispatched before they settled — is what would need an ADR.
