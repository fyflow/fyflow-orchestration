**Evaluated by P32 on 2026-10-13.** `spec/join-policy.md` ships `arrival-count`, `live-token` and
`live-token-requiring-arrival`, and all three are `satisfied` only once every opened branch has arrived,
exited or been lost. A race (*the first of three providers to answer*) or a quorum (*two of three
reviewers*) has no policy.

**The policy itself is the easy half** — a `step` that is `satisfied` at the Nth arrival is a few lines
against `JP-R3`. **The hard half is the losers.** They cannot be cancelled while queued
([S2](../scheduler-upstream.md)), so they settle after the join fired: whether those settlements are
recorded, routed, or reach the join's successor's `JP-R12` collection — and what `FD-C1`'s *the value
this node receives* is for a successor dispatched before they settled — is what would need an ADR.

**2026-10-17, P34's retro ((l), answered by the maintainer as recommended): the premise above is out of date.** A queued
task CAN be cancelled since the 0.6.0 pin — [S2](../scheduler-upstream.md) shipped as `scheduler.cancel(id)` — and a
run's owner cancels the whole run with `driver.cancel` since [P34](../plans/p34-what-cancelling-stopping-and-pausing-a-run-do.md)
([ADR 0104](../decisions/0104-a-run-its-owner-cancels-is-cancelling-until-its-last-task-has-stopped.md)). So the losers
need not run on; but a loser cancelled by its id settles `cancelled`, and that ends its RUN under `RN-R28` (ADR 0094). The
hard half is therefore unchanged in kind: what a loser's settlement after the join fired does — now including a
`cancelled` one. Still deferred.

**2026-10-19, P38's retro: the early-firing SHAPE is already reachable, and it has now bitten once.** No shipped policy
fires early, but a consumer's policy through `StartOptions.joinPolicy` can — and [W191](../work-items/w191-the-map-instances-item-as-a-reference.md)'s
conformity reader built one on a join NESTED in a `map` instance: the instance's branch arrived while a nested branch
could still enter a node reading `"$item"`, the item had been released, and the run HALTED (its C1, a BLOCKER). Fixed by
[ADR 0118](../decisions/0118-a-map-instance-keeps-its-item-while-a-node-of-it-may-still-be-entered.md) rule 5's horizon —
the instance's branch terminal AND no branch of a region nested in it live — and exercised under an early-firing policy at
L2 (`test/driver/map-item.test.ts`) and on real workers (W192's clause (6)). **A retention rule that assumes every join
waits for all of its branches is the hazard this question names, met from the inside**; the losers' half is unchanged.
Still deferred.
