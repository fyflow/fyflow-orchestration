**Raised by [W158](../work-items/w158-cancelled-is-a-library-state-and-a-run-outcome.md)'s two readers** (the
conformity reader's F7b, the adequacy reader's F3 — one shared subject). `src/scheduler/runtime.ts`'s
`settleIfTerminal` (`:646-651`) settles `done` and calls `router.release(runId)` when `isTerminal(state)`, and W158
makes `isTerminal` true for `cancelled` (asserted at L1). **Nothing asserts the runtime's half**: that a
`task.cancelled` routed by `createRuntime` (ADR 0088) reaches the driver, that `done` resolves with
`outcome: 'cancelled'`, and that the run is then released (ADR 0091 rule 1). `test/scheduler/runtime.test.ts:2687`'s
*"rule 1 on every door and outcome"* case covers `failed` and a resumed run and has no `cancelled` row.

Two owners close it: P34, whose cancel of a run lives on the runtime, adds the no-worker case; and
[P33](../plans/p33-what-a-run-does-when-a-task-ends-cancelled.md)'s W160, whose L6 rows cancel on real workers,
awaits `done` rather than only folding the sequence.
