**Raised by [W158](../work-items/w158-cancelled-is-a-library-state-and-a-run-outcome.md)'s two readers** (the
conformity reader's F1, the adequacy reader's F2 — one shared subject). `RN-R28` says a cancel halts the run as
`OR-R10`'s stop does, and the driver checks the halt at `settle`, at `resolve()`'s entry and in `settleJoin` after
its append. **Two doors re-check nothing after a re-entrant callback can have halted the run:**

| door | where | what leaks |
|---|---|---|
| (a) a fan-out's dispatch loop | `src/driver/driver.ts` — the `region.branches.forEach(… enter …)` at `:2372` | a consumer's `onFoldInput` that cancels synchronously on branch 1's `dispatched` element (`cancel()` of a WAITING task fires `task.cancelled` inside the call) halts the run, and branch 2 is **still dispatched** |
| (b) `resolve()` after its append | `:1809-1843` — `appendResolution` → `branch.resumed` → `advance` | a cancel raised from `onFoldInput` on the resolution's own element halts the run, and the `branch.resumed` and the routing after it **still run** |

**The stop has the same leak** (the readers' control): a refusal raised by a re-entrant callback at either door
sets `failed` and routing continues the same way. So it is not a cancel defect but a property of every halt
raised on the stack, and the maintainer's decision is to close it once, for both, with
[P34](../plans/p34-what-cancelling-stopping-and-pausing-a-run-do.md)'s dispatch hold (its decision 5) — the one
mechanism every halt is to share — rather than to patch the cancel alone in W158.

**What is true meanwhile**, and is what `RN-R28` and ADR 0094 now say: a halt raised from a settlement or a
resolution at rest dispatches nothing further; one raised re-entrantly on these two stacks can let one more
dispatch or one more routing through. The fold still reads the run `cancelled` (or `stopped`, the name 0.7.0 gave the halt — `halted` since W171, ADR 0102) — the
extra work's settlement is appended and routed nowhere.

**Closed 2026-10-16 by [W172](../work-items/w172-the-dispatch-hold-and-q605.md), [ADR 0103](../decisions/0103-every-halt-shares-one-dispatch-hold.md).**
*"A stop"* in this question and its `closes-when` is the meaning it had when raised — today's HALT on a defect (`OR-R10`,
`halted` since W171), not P34's resumable stop. Both doors are shut for it and for a cancelled settlement, each asserted
beside its control at L2. P34's requested cancel and its stop (W173, W174) are covered by JOINING `shut()`, which
those items add and verify at their own gates; nothing here asserts them.
