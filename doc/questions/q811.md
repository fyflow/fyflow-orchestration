| 7 | `src/driver/driver.ts`, `loseLoop` and `fireLoop` | both re-implement `settleJoin`'s unsatisfiable and satisfied arms: the `firedJoins` latch, `joinSettled`, `onUnsatisfiableJoin`, `JP-R10`'s `branch.lost` for the enclosing region, the reading's `offer`, the successor's `enter`.
