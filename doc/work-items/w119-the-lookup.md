## Scope

**In**

1. **Filters**: `--where field=value` exact, `--where field~text` case-insensitive substring,
   repeatable and ANDed, over **any** front matter field of the record kind. `--search text` is a
   case-insensitive substring over every text field.
2. **Projection**: `--fields` prints only the named fields, in the order named. Defaults are the
   cheap ones — a list prints `id`, `status`, `title`; a single record prints its front matter and
   nothing derived. **`refs` and `body` are never printed unless named.**
3. **`icebox`**: left out of every list and every search unless `--where status=icebox` or
   `--include-icebox` is given. **By id it is returned**, because naming it is seeking it. When the
   exclusion hides any match, the output's last line says how many and how to see them.
4. **Exit codes are grep's.** 0: at least one record. 1: none — and the line names the filter or
   the id that found nothing, plus any hidden `icebox` count. **2: the reading is broken** — an
   unknown field in `--where` or `--fields`, an unparseable record, a kind that parses to zero
   records.
   *An unknown field is a 2 and never an empty result*: a misspelt field that silently matches
   nothing is `state.mjs`'s founding defect.
5. `refs` matches an id as a whole token (`Q17` does not match `Q170`), excludes `dev-dist/`,
   `node_modules/` and `.git/`, and prints paths with counts, never lines.
6. `adr --index` reproduces the columns the hand-kept index had, from front matter alone. **Before
   that section is deleted the two are compared and every difference is listed in the Record** —
   each is either a wrong hand-kept row or a wrong header, and the Record says which.

**Out**

- Writing anything. The lookup reads; `check:register` judges.
- Plans and work items as record kinds — P28 *Out*. `refs` still finds citations of their ids.
- Ranking or fuzzy search. Substring is predictable, and a ranked answer is a claim about relevance
  nobody checks.
